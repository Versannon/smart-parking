# Parkora — Demo Video Script & Recording Guide

Use this guide when recording your website demonstration video. It includes exact timestamps, visual screen actions, word-for-word voiceover script, and recording tips.

---

## 📹 Video Setup & Recording Recommendations

- **Target Duration**: ~2 Minutes (120 to 140 seconds)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD, 60 FPS)
- **Browser Settings**: Fullscreen (`F11`), Chrome or Edge in dark mode, 100% zoom.
- **Recording Tool**: OBS Studio, Loom, or Windows Game Bar (`Win + Alt + R`).
- **Pacing**: Speak clearly at a moderate, enthusiastic speed. Allow mouse movements to look natural and deliberate.

## 💡 Quick Tips for a Standout Demo Video

1. **Mouse Cursor**: Keep mouse movements smooth. Avoid fast, erratic circling.
2. **Audio**: Use a quiet room and pause for 0.5s between scenes for clean cuts if editing.
3. **Show Off Features**: Make sure to click the filter tabs and complete at least one reservation so the confetti effect is clearly visible on camera!
